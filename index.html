InvoicePick Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

InvoicePick is a local-first Android application. This policy describes the current version of the app.

What InvoicePick stores

InvoicePick stores business profiles, client details, catalog items, invoices, estimates, payments, expenses, settings, receipt images, and recognized receipt text locally on the user's device. Structured data is stored in the app's local database, and receipt images are copied to app-private storage.

On-device OCR

Receipt text recognition is performed on the device using bundled OCR models. Receipt images and recognized text are not sent to an InvoicePick server for processing.

Camera and photo access

InvoicePick requests camera permission only when the user chooses to photograph a receipt. Users can also select an existing receipt image through Android's system photo picker. The app does not request broad photo, media, or storage access.

Network and data transmission

The current release does not request Android's INTERNET permission and has no InvoicePick cloud backend. InvoicePick does not automatically transmit business, invoice, expense, receipt, or OCR data off the device.

InvoicePick Pro purchases are processed by Google Play. InvoicePick uses Google Play Billing to request localized product information and to query purchase and entitlement status. Google Play, rather than InvoicePick, processes the payment transaction. InvoicePick does not directly process or store payment card details, and it does not send receipt, OCR, invoice, expense, or business content to an InvoicePick server for Billing.

When a user deliberately shares a PDF or other file through Android's system sharing interface, the selected destination app or service receives that file at the user's direction. The destination's own privacy practices apply.

Analytics and advertising

InvoicePick currently contains no analytics SDK and no advertising SDK.

Accounts

InvoicePick does not require or provide an account, login, or signup system.

Data retention and deletion

App data remains in InvoicePick's private storage until the user deletes supported records or uninstalls the app. Uninstalling InvoicePick may delete its local database, receipt images, recognized receipt text, and other app-private files. Files the user has separately shared, exported, or backed up are outside InvoicePick's private storage and are not removed by uninstalling the app.

InvoicePick disables Android automatic cloud backup for its core app data.

Free OCR quota counters and a cached Pro entitlement are stored locally. Clearing app data or uninstalling may reset the local Free quota state. When available, InvoicePick rechecks lifetime Pro ownership with Google Play.

Children's privacy

InvoicePick is a business productivity application and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not operate an account service or collect app data on an InvoicePick server.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if a future feature changes how InvoicePick accesses, processes, stores, or transmits data. The updated policy will show a revised date.
